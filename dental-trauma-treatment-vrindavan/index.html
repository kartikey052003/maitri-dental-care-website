Frequently asked about dental trauma

What should I do immediately after a dental injury?

Stay calm. If a tooth is knocked out, handle it by the crown (not the root), rinse it gently with milk or saline, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Call the clinic immediately and describe the injury. We can advise the next steps and may ask you to come in urgently.

Can a knocked-out tooth be saved?

The chances of saving a knocked-out tooth are highest if it is handled carefully and replaced quickly (ideally within 30 minutes to 2 hours). This is why immediate action and professional assessment are critical. Call us right away.

How is a broken tooth treated?

Treatment depends on the severity. A small chip might need smoothing or a filling. A deeper break might need a crown, root canal, or other treatment. The dentist will examine and advise after assessment.

Will my tooth look normal after treatment?

Modern dentistry can restore most dental injuries to look and function well. The exact outcome depends on the injury. The dentist will explain your options after examining you.
